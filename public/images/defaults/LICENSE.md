# Default pictures

Pictures shown when a member, group or event has none of its own:

- `user.svg`: member avatar
- `group.svg`: group picture
- `group-cover.svg`: group cover
- `event-cover.svg`: event cover

They were drawn for AnahnuClub from plain shapes (circles, rectangles, paths), with no third party artwork, and are released into the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may copy, change and use them for any purpose, including commercial, without asking and without attribution.
